About

A little about me

Hi, I'm Nancy. I'm a product manager and recent graduate of the University of Pennsylvania's School of Engineering and Applied Science, where I studied Computer Science. I moved to Hong Kong in September 2026 under the Top Talent Pass Scheme and I'm so excited to be here.

I'm passionate about using AI to build projects that make life better for people.

I also care deeply about creating community, because as AI becomes more and more capable of handling technical tasks, our ability to connect and communicate with each other becomes increasingly important.

As a student, I was President of CIT59x, the student body organization of my master's program. I oversaw the organizing of all social events, career programming, student advocacy, alumni relations, trips, and the annual Hackathon for my program. You can check out some of our going-ons here.

In my free time, I enjoy creating pottery and running road races. I've completed 6 half marathons, the Cherry Blossom 10-miler, and many other shorter races.
